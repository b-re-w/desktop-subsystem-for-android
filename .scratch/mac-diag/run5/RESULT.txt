Where they are: only Z3 is inside zram (freeing a slot whose zsmalloc handle is garbage). None
is in zram write/compress, in swap-in, in free page reporting or in the balloon. The victims
are unrelated objects: a VMA (three times: smaps, munmap, rmap), an xarray node, a vmalloc
mapping, a zsmalloc handle, two rss counters. That is the picture of memory being written or
reused after free by something else, with whoever touches it next dying; not of one faulty path.
Three of five panics came 75 to 103 s after boot, within a few seconds of the first large
trims after "Android has settled after its boot".

WHAT THE SPLITS RULE OUT
- The lz4 compressor: panics with lzo-rle (2 of 4). A zram reset also drops the recompress
  algorithm (recomp_algorithm reads empty after it), so Z ran without zstd recompression too.
- zram idle marking, recompression and compaction: K2 panicked with all three writes failing.
- Free page reporting as the source: reported_total_kb and reported_kb read 0 on this host
  before anything was changed, nr_balloon_pages 0. Nothing is reported on the Mac, with or
  without the balloon. /sys/module/page_reporting/parameters has no switch to turn it off
  (delay_ms, fault_cluster_order, page_reporting_order, rereport, counters); unbinding
  virtio8 from virtio_balloon was the method used for P.
- Proactive reclaim by itself: T and I pushed anonymous memory to zram through memory.reclaim
  for 15 minutes (swap use 250-330 MiB, like the controls) and A reclaimed page cache without
  swap, 0 of 10. MADV_PAGEOUT by dsa-channeld ("paged out ... after it woke") ran 12-23 times
  in every boot, clean ones included.
NOT SEPARATED
- What init's own trigger does that T and I do not: sizes chosen by dsa-channeld (157 MiB in
  one trim against 64), "drop_caches 3", "page_reporting rereport 1", and the timing against
  the boot. In B, T and I the full reclaim trigger still ran 1-3 times per boot (drop_caches,
  zram writes, rereport), against 1-12 in the controls, so those are reduced, not removed.
- Real "only the idle reclaim blocked" / "only the trim blocked": init's writes cannot be told
  apart at the bind mount, so T and I are synthetic writers, not dsa-channeld's own schedule.
